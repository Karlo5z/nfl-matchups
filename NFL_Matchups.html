NFL Matchups

How to read this
  • What a defense allows. Yards and touchdowns per game to the offenses it has faced this season. Rank 1 means it allows the most. A defense ranked 1st against QBs is the best matchup for a quarterback.
  • Colors. green means the defense allows a lot (ranks 1 to 8, good for the offense). red means it allows very little (ranks 25 to 32).
  • Line is what sportsbooks are asking for that player's yards today (the middle of about ten books). 3-game avg is what he has actually done in Weeks 1 to 3. Our proj is the model's number, which runs low for WRs and TEs.
  • Attack the matchup favors going over. Careful the matchup works against it. Watch mixed signals or an injury to check first.
